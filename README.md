# Awesome 3D Drawing [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

3D drawing papers and related resources on using motion-tracked controllers in XR to create art and animation. Combined with ML processing and rendering of frame-by-frame volumetric representations, these approaches are yielding results of increasing interest to mainstream animation and VFX production.

## Papers
### 3D Drawing Implementations
- [3DrawAgent: Teaching LLM to Draw in 3D with Early Contrastive Experience](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08042) A generalized VLM-based 3D drawing agent. - Hongcan Xiao et al (2026). {[code](https://github.com/Qi-CVLab/3DrawAgent)}
- [WIR3D: Visually-Informed and Geometry-Aware 3D Shape Abstraction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04813) Abstracts 3D shapes using sparse Bezier curves guided by CLIP activations and a neural SDF loss to represent geometry and visual features. - Richard Liu et al (2025). {[code](https://github.com/threedle/wir3d)}
- [3Doodle: Compact Abstraction of Objects with 3D Strokes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03690) Generates view-consistent 2D sketches from multi-view images by optimizing 3D stroke primitives like Bezier curves and superquadric contours. - Changwoon Choi et al (2024). {[code](https://github.com/changwoonchoi/3Doodle)}
- [NEF: Neural Edge Fields for 3D Parametric Curve Reconstruction from Multi-View Images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.07653) Reconstructs 3D parametric curves from multi-view images using a differentiable rendering-based optimization inspired by NeRF. - Yunfan Ye et al (2023). {[code](https://github.com/yunfan1202/NEF_code)}
- [Learning to Generate Line Drawings That Convey Geometry and Semantics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.12691) An unpaired image-to-image translation method that uses geometry and semantic losses to generate line drawings from arbitrary photographs. - Caroline Chan et al (2022). {[code](https://github.com/carolineec/informative-drawings)}
- [Towards Light-weight and Real-time Line Segment Detection](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.00186) An extremely efficient realtime and lightweight line segment detector for resource-constrained environments. - Geonmo Gu et al (2022). {[code](https://github.com/navervision/mlsd)} {[code2](https://github.com/keijiro/MlsdBarracuda)}
- [GPU-Driven Real-Time Mesh Contour Vectorization](https://diglib.eg.org/bitstream/handle/10.2312/sr20221159/093-105.pdf) A parallel GPU-based mesh contour rendering method that rasterizes, traces, and segments contour edges into 2D strip meshes for realtime stylized rendering of dense 3D models. - Wangziwei Jiang et al (2022). {[code](https://github.com/JiangWZW/Realtime-GPU-Contour-Curves-from-3D-Mesh)}
- [CASSIE: Curve and Surface Sketching in Immersive Environments](https://www-sop.inria.fr/reves/Basilic/2021/YASBS21/CASSIE_author_version.pdf) A VR modeling system that combines freehand mid-air sketching with a 3D optimization framework to create connected curve network armatures and surface patches. - Emilie Yu et al (2021). {[code](https://gitlab.inria.fr/D3/cassie)}
- [Lifting Freehand Concept Sketches into 3D](https://repo-sam.inria.fr/d3/Lift3D/Gryaditskaya_SigAsia20_Lifting%20_Freehand_Concept_Sketches_into_3D.pdf) An algorithm that automatically lifts vector-format industrial design sketches into 3D by inferring intended 3D intersections and depths from construction lines and artist strokes. - Yulia Gryaditskaya et al (2020). {[code](https://github.com/ygryadit/LiftConceptSketches3D)}
- [Line Rendering of 3D Meshes for Data-Driven Sketch-Based Modeling](https://www-sop.inria.fr/reves/Basilic/2019/WB19b/bwailly_JFIGRV_2019.pdf) A non-photorealistic rendering system that extracts contours, creases, and ridges from 3D meshes as annotated vector graphics to generate training data for sketch-based modeling. - Bastien Wailly and Adrien Bousseau (2019). {[code](https://gitlab.inria.fr/D3/contour-detect)}
- [Active Strokes: Coherent Line Stylization for Animated 3D Models](https://inria.hal.science/hal-00693453/en) Creates coherently animated line drawings by using active contours to approximate and track lines in a 3D scene for stylized rendering. - Pierre Bénard et al (2012). {[code](https://github.com/benardp/ActiveStrokes)}

### 3D Drawing Techniques and Analysis
- [PainterBench: A Figural Divergent-Thinking Benchmark for Tool-Using Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34195) A "creativity" rating methodology for agent drawings. - Shane K.A. Dalumura Hettige and Jonas Oppenlaender (2026).
- [Painting with 3D Gaussian Splat Brushes](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3721238.3730724) An interactive stamp-based painting system that enables artists to select, remix, and seamlessly blend 3D Gaussian splat brushes onto 3D scenes and surfaces. - Karran Pandey et al (2025).
- [BrushGaussian: Brushstroke-Based Stylization for 3D Gaussian Splatting](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/15/12/6881) A system that enhances 3D Gaussian Splatting primitives with texture features and unsupervised clustering to enable both color transfer and brushstroke-inspired local geometric stylization. - Zhi-Zheng Xiang et al (2025).
- [An Inverse Procedural Modeling Pipeline for Stylized Brush Stroke Rendering](https://diglib.eg.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/f12e87c6-6eda-4376-b77a-71eb945ebd8a/content) An inverse procedural modeling pipeline that uses patch segmentation and deep learning to predict procedural brush parameters and render stylized strokes from a single sample drawing. - Hao Li et al (2024).
- [Surface-Filling Curve Flows via Implicit Medial Axes](https://www.dgp.toronto.edu/projects/surface-filling-curves/surface-filling-curves.pdf) A fast, robust, and user-controllable algorithm that generates surface-filling curves on arbitrary 3D surfaces through the gradient flow of a sparse medial-axis energy. - Yuta Noma et al (2024).
- [Artists' Perspectives on Natural Interactions for Virtual Reality 3D Sketching](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3613904.3642758) A study investigating artists' perspectives on VR 3D sketching to identify natural unimodal and multimodal interaction techniques that expand beyond standard controller-based mark-making. - Richard Rodriguez et al (2024).
- [CLIPasso: Semantically-Aware Object Sketching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.05822) An object sketching method that utilizes CLIP to generate Bézier curve drawings at various levels of abstraction while maintaining semantic recognizability. - Yael Vinker et al (2022).
- [CLIPDraw: Exploring Text-to-Drawing Synthesis through Language-Image Encoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.14843) A text-to-drawing synthesis algorithm that optimizes vector strokes to match natural language descriptions using a pre-trained CLIP encoder without requiring additional training. - Kevin Frans et al (2021).
- [HandPainter: 3D Sketching in VR with Hand-based Physical Proxy](https://yingjiang96.github.io/handpaintermaterial/handpainter.pdf) A glove-based VR sketching system that improves drawing precision without controllers or tablets by using one hand as a physical canvas proxy and the other hand's index finger as a 3D pen. - Ying Jiang et al (2021).
- [Adaptive General and Predictable VR Ribbon Brush](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/labs/imager/tr/2021/AdaptiBrush/) A VR ribbon brush system that introduces an adaptive ruling direction computation to enable comfortable and predictable drawing of complex 3D ribbon geometries. - Enrique Rosales et al (2021).
- [Why Do Line Drawings Work? A Realism Hypothesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.06260) Proposes a realism hypothesis stating that the human visual system recognizes object identity and shape in line drawings by perceiving them as approximately realistic images. - Aaron Hertzmann (2020).
- [Neural Contours: Learning to Draw Lines from 3D Shapes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.10333) A method for generating line drawings from 3D models by combining geometric and view-based reasoning through a neural module trained on crowdsourced comparisons. - Difan Liu et al (2020).
- [The Effect of Spatial Ability on Immersive 3D Drawing](https://vvise.iat.sfu.ca/pubs/machuca2019spatial) Analysis of how the spatial abilities of users affect shape accuracy when drawing 3D sketches in VR. - Mayra Barrera-Machuca et al (2019).
- [Experimental Evaluation of Sketching on Surfaces in VR](https://www.research.autodesk.com/app/uploads/2023/03/experimental-evaluation-of-sketching.pdf_recSPFZ4RbLaE2Uio.pdf) Controlled user studies evaluating how physical drawing surfaces and visual guidance affect positional accuracy and stroke aesthetics when 3D sketching in VR. - Rahul Ahora et al (2017).
- [A Neural Representation of Sketch Drawings](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.03477) A recurrent neural network capable of generating stroke-based vector drawings of common objects across hundreds of classes using conditional and unconditional generation frameworks. - David Ha and Douglas Eck (2017).
- [Drawing on Air: Input Techniques for Controlled 3D Line Illustration](https://cs.brown.edu/research/pubs/pdfs/2007/Keefe-2007-DOA.pdf) Haptic-aided one-handed drag drawing and two-handed tape drawing techniques to give artists fine control when illustrating 3D curves in space. - Daniel Keefe et al (2007).
- [Springs and Constraints for 3D Drawing](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/228584605_Springs_and_constraints_for_3D_drawing) Haptically mediated 3D drawing and sculpting using dynamic physical models and virtual spatial constraints, demonstrated through the Dynasculpt and Griddraw prototypes. - Scott Snibbe et al (1998).
- [Holosketch: A Virtual Reality Sketching/Animation Tool](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/210079.210087) A VR 3D geometry creation and manipulation tool. - Michael Deering (1995).

### Related 3D Processing Techniques
- [Vox2Vox 3D-GAN for Brain Tumour Segmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.13653) A 3D volume-to-volume Generative Adversarial Network for segmentation of brain tumours, generating realistic segmentation outputs from multi-channel 3D MR images. - Marco Domenico Cirillo et al (2020). {[code](https://github.com/mdciri/Vox2Vox)} {[code2](https://github.com/enochkan/vox2vox)}
- [Image-to-Image Translation with Conditional Adversarial Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.07004) A general-purpose solution to image-to-image translation problems using conditional adversarial networks. - Phillip Isola et al (2016).
- [Artistic Style Transfer for Videos](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.08610) An approach that transfers the style from one image to a whole video sequence, generating consistent and stable stylized video sequences, even in cases with large motion and strong occlusion. - Manuel Ruder et al (2016).
- [Fast and Robust Edge Extraction in Unorganized Point Clouds](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/7371262) A fast and robust method for detecting sharp edge features in unorganized 3D point clouds by analyzing the covariance matrix eigenvalues of each point's k-nearest neighbors. - Dena Bazazian et al (2015). {[code](https://github.com/denabazazian/Edge_Extraction)}
- [A Neural Algorithm of Artistic Style](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.06576) A neural algorithm that uses Deep Neural Networks to separate and recombine the content and style of images, enabling the creation of high-quality artistic imagery. - Leon Gatys et al (2015).
- [3D Colour Object Reconstruction Based on Growing Neural Gas](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/6889546) An unsupervised method for reconstructing colored 3D triangular meshes from raw point clouds by extending the Growing Neural Gas network with color integration and modified Competitive Hebbian Learning. - Sergio Orts-Escolano et al (2014). {[code](https://github.com/rendchevi/growing-neural-gas)}
- [A Fast Parallel Algorithm for Thinning Digital Patterns](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/357994.358023) A fast two-subiteration parallel thinning algorithm that reduces digital patterns to unitary-thickness skeletons while preserving endpoints and pixel connectivity. - Tongjie Zhang and Ching Yee Suen (1984). {[code](https://github.com/LingDong-/skeleton-tracing)}

### Related Mocap and Volcap
- [Sparse Camera Volumetric Video Applications: A Comparison of Visual Fidelity, User Experience, and Adaptability](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/signal-processing/articles/10.3389/frsip.2025.1405808/full) An evaluation of the visual fidelity, user experience, and technical capabilities of various sparse camera volumetric video capture applications using RGBD sensors. - Christopher Remde et al (2025).
- [Skeleton-Aware 3D Human Shape Reconstruction From Point Clouds](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ICCV_2019/papers/Jiang_Skeleton-Aware_3D_Human_Shape_Reconstruction_From_Point_Clouds_ICCV_2019_paper.pdf) An end-to-end deep learning framework that incorporates skeleton-awareness to reconstruct 3D human shapes from point clouds using the SMPL parametric model. - Haiyong Jiang et al (2019).
- [AMASS: Archive of Motion Capture As Surface Shapes](https://files.is.tue.mpg.de/black/papers/amass.pdf) A comprehensive database that unifies multiple human motion capture datasets into a common framework using the SMPL body model to provide a large-scale resource for training deep learning models. - Naureen Mahmood et al (2019). {[code](https://github.com/nghorbani/amass)}
- [Hybrid Skeleton Driven Surface Registration for Temporally Consistent Volumetric Video](https://cvssp.org/projects/4d/HSDSR/Regateiro_3DV2018.pdf) A method for high-fidelity surface reconstruction of dynamic scenes by combining sparse multi-view video and depth data with a coarse-to-fine optimization approach. - Joao Regateiro et al (2018).
- [LiveScan3D: A Fast and Inexpensive 3D Data Acquisition System for Multiple Kinect v2 Sensors](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/308807023_Livescan3D_A_Fast_and_Inexpensive_3D_Data_Acquisition_System_for_Multiple_Kinect_v2_Sensors) An open-source system that enables realtime 3D data acquisition and dynamic scene reconstruction using multiple arbitrarily placed depth sensors. - Marek Kowalski et al (2015). {[code](https://github.com/BuildingVolumes/LiveScan3D)}

### Related Dissertation / Thesis
- [Designing Tools for 3D Content Authoring Based on 3D Sketching](https://theses.hal.science/tel-04484971v1) Explores 3D strokes as an expressive representation for 3D content authoring, proposing systems for shape reconstruction, VR painting, and hand-drawn animation integration. - Emilie Yu (2023).
- [Creative Visual Expression in Immersive 3D Environments](https://utoronto.scholaris.ca/items/e41e9604-a6ed-40f2-891d-222c212f4cb3) Proposes a human-centered design approach for immersive 3D creation tools, introducing four specific applications for sketching, modeling, and animation in virtual and augmented reality. - Rahul Arora (2021).
- [The Design of Playful and Intelligent Creative Tools](https://utoronto.scholaris.ca/items/30b67df3-138b-4f3f-8304-6e22bbc4b449) Introduces playful and intelligent creative interfaces that utilize machine learning and domain understanding to enhance artistic tasks like color interaction and 2D animation. - Maria Shugrina (2021).
- [Interactive 3D Sketching in Virtual Reality](https://em-yu.github.io/media/papers/MSc_Thesis__Emilie_Yu_v3.pdf) Addresses precision and multi-view visualization challenges in VR 3D sketching by combining automatic stroke beautification with surface inference to provide occlusion cues. - Emilie Yu (2020).
- [First Principles of Line Drawings](https://dspace.mit.edu/bitstream/handle/1721.1/139322/Chan-cmchan-SM-EECS-2021-thesis.pdf) Presents an unsupervised image translation method that generates stylistically diverse line drawings from photographs or 3D models by encoding 3D geometry and semantic meaning. - Caroline Chan (2017).
- [Dynamic Drawing: Broadening Practice and Participation in Procedural Art](https://www.media.mit.edu/publications/dynamic-drawing-broadening-practice-and-participation-in-procedural-art/) Introduces Para and Dynamic Brushes, two systems that integrate manual and procedural creation to make procedural art more accessible and expressive for traditional artists. - Jennifer Jacobs (2017).
- [Methods for Artistic Stylization in 3D Animation](https://cgl.ethz.ch/Downloads/Publications/Dissertations/Schm12.pdf) Presents a system for authoring and rendering painterly 3D animations, introducing methods for 3D brush stroke stamping, depth-order resolution, and embedding strokes onto implicit 3D canvases. - Johannes Schmid (2012).
- [Image Segmentation for Stylized Non-Photorealistic Rendering and Animation](https://www.dgp.toronto.edu/~alexk/segnpr.html) Presents a method for non-photorealistic rendering and animation that uses spectral clustering to segment 3D scenes into 2D primitives for applying artistic styles. - Alexander Kolliopoulos (2005).
- [3D Interfaces for Spatial Construction](https://thesis.caltech.edu/2127/1/phd.pdf) Explores 3D spatial interfaces that combine direct hand-traced surface drawing with tangible tools for constructing and manipulating freeform 3D shapes in immersive environments. - Steven Schkolne (2004).
- [Algorithms for Rendering in Artistic Styles](https://cs.nyu.edu/media/publications/hertzmann_aaron.pdf) Presents non-photorealistic rendering algorithms for generating hand-painted and hand-drawn imagery, encompassing procedural brush stroke placement, energy-minimizing relaxation, and example-based image analogies. - Aaron Hertzmann (2001).
- [Painterly Interfaces for Audiovisual Performance](https://acg.media.mit.edu/people/golan/thesis/thesis300.pdf) Introduces a painterly interface metaphor and five interactive synthesis systems that enable the realtime, simultaneous creation and manipulation of dynamic imagery and sound in a free-form space. - Golan Levin (2000).
- [Computer Rotoscoping with the Aid of Color Recognition](https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/71031?show=full) Investigates computer rotoscoping using optical videodiscs and color recognition to enable semi-automatic tracking and selective processing of objects in animation. - Rebecca Allen (1980).
- [Interactive Computer-Mediated Animation](https://publications.csail.mit.edu/lcs/pubs/pdf/MIT-LCS-TR-061.pdf) Investigates interactive computer-mediated and picture-driven animation, demonstrating how animators can construct and control dynamic frame sequences through freehand sketches, realtime gestures, and algorithms. - Ronald Baecker (1969). 
- [The Graphics Symbiosis System](https://etd.ohiolink.edu/acprod/odb_etd/r/etd/search/10?p10_accession_num=osu1486740394721916) Presents the Graphics Symbiosis System, an interactive mini-computer animation graphics language designed for habitability and extensibility. - Thomas A. DeFanti (1973).
- [Sketchpad: A Man-Machine Graphical Communication System](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1461551.1461591) Introduces Sketchpad, a pioneering graphical communication system that enables users to draw and manipulate constrained geometric figures directly on a computer display using a light pen. - Ivan Sutherland (1963).

### Related Graphics History
- [The Media Archaeology of the Sandin Image Processor](https://www.isea-symposium-archives.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/2023_Long_Copy-It-Right_The_Distribution_Religion.pdf) Examines the open-source preservation history of the analog Sandin Image Processor through media archaeology and explores reimplementing its hardware modules as digital software. - Amanda Long (2023). {[code](https://github.com/amandalong/Sandin-Image-Processor)}
- [SuperPaint: An Early Frame Buffer Graphics System](https://www.computer.org/csdl/magazine/an/2001/02/man2001020032/13rRUyft7wz) Recounts the early 1970s development of SuperPaint at Xerox PARC, a pioneering pixel-based frame buffer graphics and digital video painting system. - Richard Shoup (2001).
- [Dinosaur Input Device](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/223904.223943) Presents a stop-motion-inspired animation system that uses a physical armature covered with joint-orientation sensors to pose and animate articulated 3D computer graphics figures. - Brian Knep et al (1995).
- [The CAVE: Audio Visual Experience Automatic Virtual Environment](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/129888.129892) Introduces the CAVE, a room-scale virtual reality system that surrounds a head-tracked viewer with rear-projected stereoscopic images on its walls and floor. - Carolina Cruz-Neira et al (1992).
- [A Color Animation System Based on the Multiplane Technique](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/965141.563871) Describes an animation package developed at the Cornell Program of Computer Graphics. The basic algorithm employed is linear or non-linear interpolation between successive pairs of key frames. - Marc Levoy (1977).
- [Interactive Skeleton Techniques for Enhancing Motion Dynamics in Key Frame Animation](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/360349.360357) Presents a system for controlling motion dynamics in keyframe animation through skeleton control. - Nestor Burtnyk and Marceli Wein (1976).

## Datasets
- [Google Scanned Objects: A High-Quality Dataset of 3D Scanned Household Items](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.11918) An open-source collection of over one thousand 3D-scanned household items designed to facilitate deep learning in robotics and computer vision simulations. - Laura Downs et al (2022). {[files](https://app.gazebosim.org/GoogleResearch/fuel/collections/Scanned%20Objects%20by%20Google%20Research)}
- [Fine-Grained VR Sketching: Dataset and Insights](https://www.computer.org/csdl/proceedings-article/3dv/2021/268800b003/1zWE3NZ5Apq) A fine-grained dataset of 1,497 3D VR chair sketches paired with 3D shapes and establishes benchmarks and insights for fine-grained 3D VR sketch-based shape retrieval. - Ling Luo et al (2021). {[files](https://cvssp.org/data/VRChairSketch/)}
- [Creative Flow+ Dataset](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/creativeflow/files/2596.pdf) A system that enables the creation of line-based character animations from a small number of keyframes by using a flow-based representation to interpolate lines. - Maria Shugrina et al (2019). {[files](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/creativeflow/)}
- [ABC: A Big CAD Model Dataset for Geometric Deep Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.06216) A large-scale collection of one million CAD models featuring parametrized curves and surfaces intended for geometric deep learning research and benchmarking. - Sebastian Koch et al (2019). {[files](https://deep-geometry.github.io/abc-dataset/)}
